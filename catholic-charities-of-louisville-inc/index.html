Rooted in Faith

At Catholic Charities of Louisville, everything we do flows from the principles of our faith. Our teachings affirm that every person is created in the image of God and therefore worthy of dignity, respect, and love. Our programs reflect this vision: welcoming the stranger, feeding the hungry, caring for families, protecting the vulnerable, and working for justice. Inspired by Jesus Christ and the saints who have gone before us, we walk alongside our neighbors—never above them—helping families and individuals move from struggle to stability.
