A Wide Range Of Programs Awaits You

Complete Playground is a 45,000-square-foot facility designed for all children of all ages and all abilities. We deeply value your trust and take our responsibility for every child—especially those with sensory sensitivities—very seriously. To ensure a calm and supportive environment, we offer sensory-friendly hours on weekend mornings, featuring reduced lighting and no background music. Our philosophy is based on the principles of neurodiversity: every child has the right to play and be happy. Our mission is to create an inclusive playground that unites neurodivergent and neurotypical children of all ages. To build a world where every child can play, thrive, and feel accepted.
