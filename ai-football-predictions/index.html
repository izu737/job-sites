🔄 The prediction pipeline

Collect the data
For each upcoming match we pull over 12 categories of live data — form, head-to-head, standings, odds, injuries and more. All data is fetched fresh from authoritative sources, typically 24–48 hours before kick-off.
Calculate derived statistics
Raw data is transformed into derived metrics: rolling averages for goals and shots, clean sheet rates, home/away splits, current form streaks and BTTS frequency.
AI analysis
A large language model analyses the full data pack with a structured methodology: it compares attack vs. defence, weights recent form more heavily than older results, scans H2H patterns, and assesses injury impact.
Spot value edges
For every market the AI outputs its own probability estimate. That model probability is compared against the bookmaker's implied probability (derived from the odds).
Recommend bets
Only bets with a clear value edge are recommended. Each tip comes with a confidence rating (1–10), suggested stake units (1–3) and a plain-English rationale.

How AI Football Predictions Works

AI Football Predictions analyses 100+ football leagues using live data from the API-Football feed. For every upcoming fixture we assemble a structured data pack — team form over the last five matches, head-to-head history, league standings, current bookmaker odds, injury reports, and derived statistics like clean-sheet rate and average goals per game. That data pack is passed to a large language model with a structured methodology prompt. The model is explicitly instructed to use only the provided data — no external knowledge, no hallucinated statistics.
