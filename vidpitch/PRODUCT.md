# Vidpitch

Async video for cold outreach. One person (the owner, Izuu) records their screen with their face in a bubble, drops a moving GIF preview of the first 3 seconds into Gmail, and the prospect watches on a share page that ends by asking them to book a call (Calendly).

## Surfaces and who uses them

| Surface | User | Mode | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share page `/v/:id` | The prospect, usually first contact, often on a phone | Persuade, quietly | Watch, trust the sender, book a call |
| Library `/` | The owner | Operate | Find a video, copy its link or email block, rename, delete |
| Recorder `/record` and the extension | The owner | Operate | Set camera and mic, record, send |
| Sign in `/login` | The owner | Operate | Get in |

## Brief

Match Loom's product design (layout, structure, Lens design tokens, interaction patterns) as closely as possible, because prospects already know and trust that pattern. Do not use Loom's name, logo or illustrations: the product is Vidpitch and the prospect must never think it is Loom.

## Non negotiables

* Every video plays on Safari and iPhone.
* The booking call to action is the point of the share page: slide in card near the end, end card, and the booking bar under the video.
* No "Book a call" button in the share page top bar (owner removed it).
