Innovative Tools for Exceptional Care

Originating in Scotland, StoriiCare has become the top software choice for care businesses in 12 countries worldwide. Our mission is to revolutionize care management with innovative, user-friendly technology, enhancing care quality globally. StoriiCare was founded in 2014 after witnessing how photographs can help recover memories for those living with dementia. After more than a year of planning, development began in early 2016. Initially focusing on social inclusion and reminiscence, it became apparent that there was a need for a solution that would also support a wider range of care operations. StoriiCare is easy to use and has often been described as a Facebook for care due to ease of use and social features.
