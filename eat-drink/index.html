About Eat.Drink.Lagos

The City Deserves
Better Critics

Lagos has more restaurants opening every month than most people can keep track of. Most of them are good. Some of them are exceptional. A few of them really should go back to the drawing board.

We tell you which is which. No fluff, no filler — just a clear-eyed assessment of whether the food is worth your time and your money.

The EDL Standard

How We Score
a Restaurant

Our rating system is built to separate the genuinely great from the merely decent. Four dimensions. One number that matters.

🍽️
Dimension One

The Food

Taste, technique, consistency, and whether you find yourself thinking about the meal the following day.

🤝
Dimension Two

The Service

Not just friendliness — attentiveness, knowledge, and whether you feel like a guest or an inconvenience.

✦
Dimension Three

The Experience

Atmosphere, pacing, value, and whether the whole evening adds up to something worth repeating.
