Our Story

Music for Every Person, Every Stage of Life

Founded in 1894, MacPhail Center for Music is one of America's oldest and largest nonprofit community music schools. Our mission is simple: to make high-quality music education accessible to everyone, regardless of age, background, or ability.

We believe that music is a universal language — one that builds creativity, discipline, confidence, and connection. From toddlers exploring rhythm for the first time to adults rekindling a childhood passion, MacPhail is the place where musical journeys begin and flourish.

  • ✓ Nonprofit community music school since 1894
  • ✓ Locations throughout the Twin Cities metro
  • ✓ Online and in-person options available
  • ✓ Need-based financial assistance available
  • ✓ Accredited by the National Association of Schools of Music
