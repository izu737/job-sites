Our Story

A Legacy Built on
the Power of Music

Founded in 1894, MacPhail Center for Music has spent more than a century dedicated to making high-quality music education accessible to all people across the Twin Cities and beyond.

From private lessons to group classes, early childhood music to adult learning, orchestral ensembles to community outreach — MacPhail is where passion meets purpose, and every student finds their voice.

With multiple locations across the Minneapolis-Saint Paul metro area, MacPhail serves thousands of students every year, connecting communities through the universal language of music.

Programs for
Every Musician

Whether you're picking up an instrument for the first time or refining decades of skill, MacPhail has a program designed for you.

🎸

Private Lessons

One-on-one instruction with expert faculty across dozens of instruments and voice. Tailored to your goals, pace, and musical style.

🎻

Group Classes & Ensembles

Orchestra, chamber music, jazz ensembles, choir, and more. Play alongside peers, build ensemble skills, and perform on stage.

👶

Early Childhood Music

Music Together® and other early learning programs for babies, toddlers, and preschoolers — and their caregivers — to explore music together.

🏫

School Partnerships

In-school and after-school programs that bring professional music instruction directly into classrooms and community centers.

🎓

Adult Learning

It's never too late to start or return to music. Adult group classes and private lessons for all experience levels and all instruments.

🎤

Summer & Intensives

Summer camps, master classes, workshops, and intensive programs that accelerate growth and ignite a lifelong love of music.

Community & Access

Music for
Everyone

MacPhail believes access to music education should never be limited by circumstance. Our community initiatives open doors for all.

Financial Access

Tuition Assistance Program

Need-based financial aid ensures that cost is never a barrier. MacPhail awards hundreds of thousands of dollars in assistance each year to students of all ages.

In-School Programs

Partnership with Schools

MacPhail partners with Minneapolis, Saint Paul, and suburban schools to deliver music education where students already are — in classrooms, libraries, and after-school programs.

Outreach

Community Ensembles

Free and low-cost ensemble opportunities for youth and adults who might not otherwise have access to performance and collaborative music-making experiences.

Adaptive Programs

Music for All Abilities

Adaptive and inclusive programs support students with disabilities, ensuring that the transformative power of music is truly available to every person in our community.
