What We Offer

World-Class Music Programs

From beginner lessons to advanced performance ensembles, MacPhail offers a full spectrum of musical experiences for learners of all ages.

🎵
Private Lessons
One-on-one instruction in piano, violin, guitar, voice, drums, and dozens more instruments — tailored to your pace and goals.
🎻
Early Childhood Music
Suzuki, Music Together, and movement-based programs that build a lifelong love of music from the very first notes — starting at birth.
🎹
Group Classes
Collaborative learning environments where students develop musicianship, theory, and performance skills alongside peers.
🎺
Ensembles & Orchestras
Youth orchestras, chamber groups, jazz ensembles, and choirs — building artistry, discipline, and the joy of making music together.
🌍
Community Programs
Reaching underserved communities through scholarships, outreach, and partnerships — because everyone deserves music education.
🎓
Adult Education
It's never too late to start or return to music. Our adult programs are designed for busy lives and lifelong learning.

Our Mission

Music for Every Person in Our Community

MacPhail is more than a music school. We are a community institution committed to equity, access, and the belief that music transforms lives at every stage.

  • ✓Scholarship and financial aid programs to ensure cost is never a barrier
  • ✓Suzuki-trained and internationally recognized faculty
  • ✓In-school and community outreach programs across Minneapolis
  • ✓State-of-the-art performance venues and recording facilities
  • ✓Flexible scheduling including in-person, hybrid, and online lessons
  • ✓Programs rooted in diversity, equity, and cultural celebration
