Our Mission

Music for Every Person, Every Stage of Life

We believe music education should be accessible to everyone. MacPhail removes barriers and creates pathways — from early childhood through adulthood — with world-class instruction and deep community roots.

🎵

Expert Instruction

Faculty of professional musicians and educators dedicated to unlocking each student's potential.

🌍

Radical Accessibility

Twin Cities Roots

Deeply embedded in the Minneapolis community with multiple campuses and in-school partnerships.
