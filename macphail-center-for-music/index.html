Our Mission

A Legacy of Musical Impact

MacPhail Center for Music connects people with the power of music through exceptional learning and community programs.

For over a century, MacPhail has been at the heart of the Twin Cities music community. We are one of the largest nonprofit community music schools in the United States, driven by a belief that music transforms lives — and that access to music should be equitable for everyone.

  • ✓
    Nonprofit, community-centered music education
  • ✓
    Need-based financial assistance and scholarships available
  • ✓
    Inclusive programs for all abilities and backgrounds
  • ✓
    World-class faculty with professional performance backgrounds
  • ✓
    State-of-the-art facilities across 5 Twin Cities locations
Our Approach

How MacPhail Transforms Lives

Our holistic approach to music education goes beyond technique — we build confidence, community, and a lifelong relationship with music.
