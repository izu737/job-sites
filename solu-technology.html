Our Story

30 Years of Solving Technology Challenges

Founded in 1995 as Croop-LaFrance, Solü Technology Partners quickly evolved into one of the premier information technology service providers in Upstate New York. In 2012, we spun off our rapidly growing commercial technology staffing and managed services division into Solü Technology Partners.

Today we are a national technology staffing and software solutions provider empowering companies and people for over 30 years. Our clients range from Fortune 500 companies we've partnered with for decades, to small and medium-sized businesses across the nation who rely on us to solve their most pressing technology challenges.

FAQ

Common Questions

What types of technology roles does Solü place? +
Solü places professionals across the full technology spectrum — including software engineers, cloud architects, DevOps engineers, network administrators, project managers, data architects, QA automation engineers, help desk specialists, and more. If it's technology, we staff it.
Do you offer contract, contract-to-hire, and permanent placements? +
Yes. Solü offers flexible engagement models including short-term contracts, long-term consulting, contract-to-direct placements, and direct permanent hire — tailored to what your business needs right now.
What industries do you serve? +
We serve clients across virtually every industry — financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, government, retail, energy, insurance, education, and technology companies. Our focus is always on your unique technology needs, not your industry vertical.
How quickly can Solü fill an open technology role? +
Speed depends on the role's complexity, but our extensive pre-vetted network of 500+ technologists means we often present qualified candidates within days of engagement. We prioritize quality AND speed — you should never have to choose.
Where does Solü operate? +
Solü is headquartered in Rochester, New York with offices in Syracuse NY, Phoenix AZ, Columbus OH, and Harrisburg PA. We place talent nationally across all 50 states, with deep local expertise in our core markets.
